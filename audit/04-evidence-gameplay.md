## Core loop and feel

[G1.1] Core verb clarity — first frame of play (`fp-05-playing-first-frame.png`): first-person view, pistol in hand, crosshair, score 0 / next 1,000, 3:00, "×1 COMBO", ammo 12 / 60, buildings 40–60 m away across an empty asphalt grid; spawn on Downtown looks at a convenience store 62 m off (first ray hit: steel at 62.3 m, `fresh.log`). The verb "wreck" is carried by the tagline, the loadout, and the first hit's debris; nothing on screen names the goal (no "destroy structures", no objective line). The loadout line "67,048 POINTS STANDING" is the one statement that structures are the score. Terrain vs structure: a 2 m / power 60 probe into the asphalt next to spawn gave `scoreDelta 0`, no `structureDamaged` event, the readout plate unchanged, debris + particles + hit marker only (`fresh.log` "terrain hit"); the same probe into the convenience store gave +1,329, the readout "CONVENIENCE STORE 26 % · 5,228 pts", the score bar flash and the combo stepping to ×1.5 (`fp-08-after-structure-hit.png`). So "terrain is free" is communicated only by the *absence* of three feedback channels, never positively. files: client/src/scoring/Score.ts:76-105, client/src/ui/hud/StructureReadout.ts:20-27, client/src/ui/hud/ScoreBar.ts:19-25.

[G1.2] Responsiveness (input path) — `Input.ts` accumulates DOM events (`keys`, `pressed`, `mouseDown`, `mousePressed`, `dx/dy`) and `Game.frame()` polls them once per frame in order: `flow.update` → `player.look(input)` → `player.update` → `weapons.update(dt, now, input)` → `destruction.update` → … → `effects.applyShake` → `gfx.render()` → `input.endFrame()` (`Game.ts:285-325`). A `mousedown` sets `mousePressed[0]` immediately and it is cleared only at `endFrame`, so a click shorter than one frame still fires. For hitscan weapons `fire()` calls `applyDamage` synchronously, which emits `hit` synchronously, so debris/flash/particles/score/readout all land in the **same frame** as the poll; measured in `weapons.log`: shotgun and rifle `frames.firstDestroyHit = 0` (same rAF as the fire), pickaxe/bat `= 2` because melee deliberately resolves after `WEAPON_TUNING.meleeSwingTime × 0.5 = 0.1 s` (`Weapons.ts:219-225`), rocket `= 3` (40 m/s projectile over 8 m). Nothing else defers the visible response; mouse-look is applied before the controller moves (`Game.ts:293-294`). Deliberate buffering/latency: melee 100 ms swing, grenade 3 s fuse, C4 waits for a second click, radial picker slows time to 0.3× while Q is held (`PLAYER.radialTimeScale`). Real input-to-photon latency and pointer-lock feel cannot be measured here. files: client/src/player/Input.ts:19-52, client/src/game/Game.ts:285-325, client/src/player/Weapons.ts:181-262.

## Progression, difficulty, flow

[G3.1] Difficulty curve — a single 180 s round with no enemies; the curve is a resource curve: fixed ammo that pays out against structures of 248–19,971 pts (`determinism.log`: Downtown AUDIT has 9 structures from 248 to 19,971 pts plus 15 props of 6–246, arena total 67,407; THEME_MAX_SCORE caps 60k/120k/60k). Early hook: the first 1,000-pt milestone (one rocket into a store is +4,035 live) arrives within the first few shots; mid-round: milestones every 1,000 pts and leveled bonuses (50 % of the structure's value); crescendo: the red pulsing clock under 30 s (`Timer.ts:11`) and the slow-mo freeze. No cliff; also no designed valley or escalation, the arena is identical at second 170 as at second 10 (no new element arrives). files: config/scoring.ts, client/src/ui/hud/Timer.ts:8-12, determinism.log.

[G3.2] Difficulty options — none: Settings has exactly three sliders (MOUSE SENSITIVITY, VOLUME, FIELD OF VIEW; `Settings.ts:35-38`); no round length, assist, aim help, or motion toggle; the only variant is the point-buy loadout mode (`Loadout.ts:28-32`), which is a kit choice, not a difficulty or assist. files: client/src/ui/screens/Settings.ts:1-44, config/loadout.ts.

[G3.3] Goal visibility — at any moment the HUD shows: score, the next milestone number and a fill toward it (`ScoreBar.ts`), the clock, the current structure's name/% for 3 s after a hit (`StructureReadout.ts`), the combo meter and boost timer. The *next* goal ("level this building", "find the propane") is never surfaced; there is no objective line, no compass to high-value structures, no arena % until the results card. `fp-08-after-structure-hit.png` shows the full set. files: client/src/ui/hud/ScoreBar.ts, StructureReadout.ts, ComboMeter.ts.

[G3.4] Reward schedule — kinds: points (continuous), combo multiplier levels (×1.5 … ×5), milestone rewards cycling +1 grenade → +1 rocket → +1 C4 → +5 s every 1,000 pts (`config/milestones.ts:21-32`, callouts cycle WRECKING / DEMOLITION / TOTAL CHAOS / RAMPAGE / UNSTOPPABLE / ANNIHILATION / APOCALYPSE), leveled bonus (+50 % of the structure's value) plus +1 magazine of the weapon in hand and +25 % power for 12 s stacking to +100 % (`LEVELED_REWARD`). It is a **fixed-interval drip** (every 1,000 pts, constant), varied in kind but not timed to an interest curve; the rewards are all "power/ammo", none cosmetic or narrative, which fits the genre. The milestone ceiling is high: a strong Downtown round crosses 60+ milestones, so the drip/quiet logic (`milestoneLoudPerBurst: 3`) was needed to stop flash spam (DECISIONS.md "Milestones drip"). files: config/milestones.ts, config/scoring.ts:46-56, client/src/scoring/Score.ts:128-146.

[G3.5] Variety cadence — within a round nothing new arrives (no new mechanic, element or twist after t=0 besides the ammo you earn). Across rounds: 3 themes × 14 structure recipes × 9 prop recipes × seeded placement (6–10 structures per arena, `placer.test.ts:27-30`). Weapon count 8 with distinct behaviours (melee ×2, hitscan ×3, projectile, thrown, placed). files: client/src/world/gen/recipes/index.ts:7-15, config/themes.ts, client/src/world/gen/placer.test.ts.

[G3.6] Session length fit — 180 s round + ~10 s of screens; a natural stop at every results screen; quitting mid-round (Esc → QUIT TO MENU) discards the round with no penalty beyond losing it (`Flow.ts:230-236`); pause stops the clock (`timeScale = 0`, `Flow.ts:216-221`).
