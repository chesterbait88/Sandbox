# Decisions

- **Docker deferred, then verified at the end.** The user asked to iterate locally first, so no phase gate depended on Docker. At the end of the run the image was built (through the sandbox's proxy with a gitignored `Dockerfile.sandbox` that only adds the proxy CA; the committed `Dockerfile` is unchanged) and run with a volume; endpoints and restart persistence checked. Runtime dependencies come from `pnpm deploy --legacy --prod`, so the image carries only the server's production modules.
- **Server is bundled with esbuild** so it can import the shared `config/` TypeScript directly; `better-sqlite3` stays external.
- **Playwright** pins 1.63.0 to match the browser preinstalled in the build sandbox; the screenshot script uses `CHROMIUM_PATH` or `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium`.
- **Gravel and Water** were added to `materials.ts` (ids 6, 7) because the Memorial Park theme calls for gravel paths and a fountain with water; both are terrain-value materials.
- **World storage is one flat `Uint8Array` + `Uint16Array`** (8.4 M voxels, ~25 MB) instead of per-chunk arrays. Padded chunk copies for the mesher become contiguous row copies, and the owner array is always allocated (simpler than lazily per chunk). `Chunk.ts` holds render state only.
- **Per-voxel colour jitter is applied in the fragment shader** from world position (stable across remeshes) rather than baked per vertex, so greedy merging still works. AO is baked into vertex colour; neon uses an `emit` vertex attribute instead of a third mesh.
- **Sidewalk** (id 8) was added as a terrain-valued concrete look-alike so Downtown sidewalks score nothing.
- **FPS claims**: the sandbox has no GPU; frame timing is measured for CPU-side work (mesher, controller, damage) and the GPU budget is kept by construction (few hundred draw calls, vertex colours only).
- **Volatile dedupe radius is 2 m** (spec said 1 m): a 1×1×2 m fuel tank detonated twice at 1 m. One detonation per tank is the intent.
- **Grenades and rockets use hand-rolled ballistics against the voxel grid** (raycast per step, reflect on bounce) instead of Rapier bodies: Rapier only knows the terrain heightfield, so a Rapier grenade would fly through every wall. Rapier is reserved for debris.
- **Bullet and melee damage is centred on the hit voxel's centre** (`snapToVoxelCentre`) so the hit voxel always receives full power and hits at voxel edges do not fizzle. Pistol power was retuned 5 → 2.5 and rifle 9 → 6 so that pistol pops glass/drywall/wood, rifle and shotgun eat brick and concrete, and nothing but explosives touches steel.
- **View-model voxels are 0.025 m, not 0.1 m**: at 0.1 m a 24-voxel gun is 2.4 m long and fills the screen. The models still read as chunkier than the world.
- **Debris has CCD off and physics takes at most 2 sub-steps per frame**; CCD on 24 fresh bodies cost 15 ms in one step. Pieces move under 0.3 m per step so tunnelling through the heightfield is not an issue.
- **A warm-up pass runs at world load** (scratch carve, Rapier steps, AudioContext creation) because the first calls were 10–50× slower than warm ones.
- **One InstancedMesh for all debris** with per-instance colour instead of one per material; same look, one draw call.
- **The terrain heightfield is rebuilt at most once a second** from the columns that craters changed (replacing the earlier MVP decision to leave it static); a rebuild costs a few ms and only runs while terrain is being dug.
- **Volatile dedupe radius is 2 m** (see above).
- **Structure recipes draw in local voxel coordinates with y = 0 on the pad**, and the placer flattens a pad to the median terrain height under the footprint (+2 m margin). Pads keep the terrain material on top so they still score nothing.
- **Placement is jittered-grid first, random second**: 36 m cells shuffled per seed, each structure tries its cell with jitter, then random spots, up to 40 attempts, 10 m gap, 10 m edge margin. The desert road and the spawn pad are reserved rectangles.
- **Counts above the pool size repeat recipes** (e.g. two gas stations) after reshuffling the pool, since the spec asks for 6–10 structures from pools of 4–5.
- **Water tower holds water and the fountain has water**: material 7 (terrain-valued, transparent) so it reads as water but pays nothing.
- **The milestone `time` reward is emitted but applied by nothing until the round clock exists (phase 4).**
- **Arcade palette (user request after phase 3).** Structure materials were re-coloured to distinct saturated hues (blue steel, red-orange brick, orange wood, cream drywall, cool concrete, pink-white marble) and volatile materials got two-tone patterns: fuel tanks are red/yellow hazard stripes, propane and transformers carry a colour band, so the explosive things read at a glance. Terrain got slightly more saturated too. All of it is data in `materials.ts` (`color2` + `stripes`/`band` patterns).
- **Voxel outlines in the shader (user request).** Each face darkens along its 0.5 m voxel edges (minimum one pixel wide via `fwidth`, fading with distance), gets a light bevel on the top/left edges and a faint diagonal hatch, all from `RENDER.outline*/bevel*/hatch*` in `config/world.ts`. Instanced debris uses the instance matrix for its world position so pieces get the same treatment.
- **Contextual siting (user request).** Every recipe declares where it belongs (`siting`): desert gas stations, diners and billboards are `roadside` (1–4 m beyond the road shoulder, mirrored so the front faces the road when south of it), the water tower is `hill`, propane cages are `beside` a station/diner/store; downtown buildings are `block` (inside a lot between sidewalk rings, front toward a street) and bus shelters `sidewalk`; park monument `center`, fountain and statue `path` (on gravel), gazebo `hill`, shed `edge`. Each kind falls back to the jittered grid so arenas never come up short. Downtown terrain now has real 8 m streets on a 32 m pitch with 2 m sidewalk rings (`CITY` in `config/themes.ts`); the parking structure shrank to fit a 20 m lot.
- **Recipes build with their front at local z = 0** and the placer mirrors them in z when the thing they face is on the other side (`StructureCtx` `mirror`).
- **Initials entry: letters always type.** The spec's W/S/A/D navigation collided with typing those letters (an end-to-end run typed "ZAC" and got "CAA"), so arrows navigate and every letter key types; Enter confirms.
- **Wheel cycles weapons, Q opens the radial.** The spec lists the wheel as a second way to open the radial; cycling is the simpler reading and the radial stays on Q.
- **World picker cards are stylised CSS art**, not live low-res renders: the live arena behind the picker rebuilds when a card is clicked, which gives the same information with one renderer.
- **Phase 4 server is in memory** (`server/src/api.ts`): same routes and shapes as the spec, no persistence; phase 5 swaps in SQLite, signed tokens and validation. An extra `GET /qualifies` route backs the results screen's "you'd need N more" line.
- **Debug jump points** (`?screen=menu|picker|loadout|hud|pause|results|initials|leaderboard`) exist only for the screenshot script; results/initials use a fake result.
- **Round time window is enforced from the server's clock** (token `startedAt` is server-issued). `VW_DEV_NO_TIME_CHECK=1` disables it so the e2e and screenshot scripts can finish a round in seconds; never set it in production.
- **Used tokens are stored in SQLite** (`used_tokens`, pruned after 20 minutes) rather than memory so a replay across a restart is still refused.
- **Rank semantics**: before submission a score ranks below equal scores (for the "you'd need N more" line); after insertion ties share a rank.
- **UI timers (countdown, round-end freeze) use wall-clock deadlines**, not accumulated frame time, because the frame loop clamps dt at 50 ms and a slow machine would stretch a 3 s countdown to 10 s.
- **Pointer lock is requested on the DEPLOY click** (the gesture) and again on any canvas click while playing; losing the lock pauses only while the clock is running.
- **Menu music is a scheduled synth loop** (`MUSIC` chords in `config/effects.ts`), started on menu screens and after the first gesture resumes the AudioContext.
- **Collapsed clusters pay points** as if destroyed (they are removed from the world and scored through the same path), so dropping a building's upper floors is rewarded. Clusters under 6 voxels are deleted silently; over 60,000 are deleted without a body.
- **Collapse meshing happens on the mesher worker** (`meshArbitrary`); the body starts falling immediately and the mesh pops in when it arrives (tens to hundreds of ms behind remeshes in the queue). One cluster is released per frame.
- **Wall colliders only for chunks that hold structure voxels**, as trimeshes built from the opaque mesh (glass has none), one per frame. Terrain stays on the heightfield.
- **Debris colliders are balls** (visual stays a cube): against trimeshes, cubes cost about twice as much on the insertion frame.
- **Milestones drip** one per 0.6 s when several are earned at once (a collapse can add 30k points in one call); after three loud callouts in a burst the rest grant quietly every 0.15 s, because thirty consecutive screen flashes washed the screen out for twenty seconds.
- **Point-buy budget is 128**, exactly the cost of the fixed kit at the per-round prices in `config/loadout.ts`; the server signs the mode into the session token so a client cannot post a point-buy score to the fixed board.
- **Share links use `?arena=theme-SEED`**; `?theme`/`?seed` remain debug parameters so screenshots and probes keep landing on the menu.
- **Band pattern is odd rows** (was every fourth row): a 2-voxel propane bottle never showed its colour band. Found via the visual gallery.
